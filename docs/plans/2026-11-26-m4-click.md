# M4: React to a click

**Status:** active · **Branch:** `m4-click` off `main` at `77b6d81` · **Started:** 2026-11-26

## Context

BRIEF.md's M4: click Kata, get a happy hop. A click on Kata puts the brain in a new `HAPPY`
state. The window plays the `happy` animation once, which is a hop with hearts. When the animator
reports it finished, Kata goes back to what the click interrupted. A click on a sleeping Kata wakes
it, and clicks while Kata is held or falling are ignored.

What shapes the plan, observed in the code:

- **The hop is drawn into the frames.** `happy` is 8 frames at 12 fps (`kata.json`), so it lasts
  8/12 s, about 0.67 s. `frames_happy` in `tools/make_sprites.py` lifts the fox up to 9 sprite
  pixels, 27 px at scale 3, inside the 48x48 frame. So the body stays on the floor and nothing in
  the physics changes.
- **The brain has no timer it could use for this.** `Brain` (`src/kata/brain.py`) times only its
  own four states. The window owns the `Animator` (`src/kata/animator.py`), whose `finished` is
  true once a one-shot's last frame has had its full time on screen. So the window tells the brain,
  the way it already reports `landed()`.
- **The window passes clicks on already.** `PetWindow.let_go` (`src/kata/window.py`) calls
  `brain.clicked()` through `think()`, which logs a `state` line on any change. So the hop and the
  return show up in KATA_LOG without new log events.
- **Today's `clicked()`** wakes a nap and otherwise only restarts the quiet spell. Two existing
  tests pin that "nothing else" and will change: `test_a_click_on_an_awake_kata_changes_nothing_it_is_doing`
  in `test_brain.py`, and M2's `test_a_press_and_release_in_place_is_a_click_and_kata_stays_put` in
  `test_window.py`. The second one expects `idle` 30 ticks after a click, and the hop lasts 43
  ticks.

Not in scope: the tray menu (M5), random happy hops with no click, and sounds.

## Decisions

From the interview (2026-11-26):

1. **Branch:** `m4-click` off `main` at `77b6d81`, merged through a pull request with a merge
   commit.
2. **A click on a sleeping Kata** wakes it into idle with no hop, as in M3. A second click hops.
3. **A click during a hop is ignored.** The hop plays to its end, and the first click after that
   starts the next one. Ten fast clicks give a few clean hops in a row, never a jump in the
   picture.
4. **Checking by hand:** I launch Kata with KATA_LOG on. You click and tell me what you saw, and I
   check each hop and each return against the log, as in M3. No slow-motion switch.

Defaults chosen without asking (approved with the plan):

- **Who ends the hop:** the animator, as BRIEF says. The brain gets a new event, `finished()`:
  "the one-shot animation has played to its end". The window sends it on the tick the animator
  reports `finished`, then plays the next animation on that same tick, so no frame is held over.
  `finished()` means nothing unless Kata is hopping, as `landed()` means nothing unless Kata is
  falling. No hop length lives in the brain or in `BehaviourConfig`, so no sprite fact is copied.
- **What a click does, by state:**
  - idle, walk or sit: a hop;
  - sleep: wakes into idle (decision 2);
  - happy: nothing (decision 3);
  - held or fall: nothing at all, not even a restart of the quiet spell, since BRIEF says these
    clicks are ignored. (Today a click while falling restarts the quiet spell.)
- **Going back** restores the interrupted state just as it was: the same state, the time it had
  already spent and the time it had left, and the same facing. A walk heading left carries on
  left, and a sit finishes the rest of its sit. The interrupted state's clock is paused while Kata
  hops, and so is the quiet spell, as they are while Kata is held. A hop draws nothing from the
  `Random`, so what Kata does after it is what it would have done without it.
- **Kata hops on the spot.** `walk_speed` is 0 during a hop, as in every state but walking, so a
  walking Kata stops, hops and walks on. A moving body would slide its paws on the hop's standing
  frames. Facing left, the hop uses the mirrored frames, which M3 already builds for every
  animation.
- **A grab or a fall cancels the hop.** `grabbed()` mid-hop goes to held, a floor dropping away
  goes to fall, and after the landing Kata stands idle. The interrupted state is forgotten.
- **`time_left` during a hop is infinite,** as while held or falling: the brain doesn't time it.
- **The window still logs `click` and emits `clicked`** for a click while Kata falls. It reports
  what the mouse did, and the brain decides what that means.
- **Double-clicks:** Qt turns the second of two quick clicks into a double-click event. Before
  relying on it, step 2 observes which events `PetWindow` actually gets for two quick clicks, and
  a test pins that each click counts once.
- **The smoke test** (the ten minutes in `test_window.py`) also clicks Kata every few seconds, so
  the hop is part of the wiring it drives. It must still take a fraction of a second.
- **Mutation:** one run after step 1, over all seven no-Qt modules, with every survivor killed or
  explained. Step 2 changes only Qt code, so step 1's run counts as the milestone's run, unless a
  later step touches a no-Qt module. Then it is run again at the close. The last full run (M3's
  close) took 2285 s; it runs in the background, and Kata isn't started while it runs.
